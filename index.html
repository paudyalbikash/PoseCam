Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PoseCam ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

PoseCam is designed to help users capture and manage photos. The App only accesses information necessary for its features.

Camera Permission

The App requests permission to access your device's camera solely to allow you to capture photographs. Photos are taken only when you choose to do so.

Photos and Media Permission

The App requests permission to access photos and media stored on your device so you can:

  • Select existing photos.
  • Save photos captured by the App.
  • Manage images created using the App.

Your photos remain on your device unless you choose to share them yourself.

Personal Information

PoseCam does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

Data Collection

PoseCam does not collect, transmit, store, or sell your personal data. Images selected or captured using the App remain on your device unless you explicitly share them using your device's sharing features.

Third-Party Services

The App may use Google Play services or other trusted third-party libraries required for app functionality. These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Security

We are committed to protecting your privacy. Since your photos are stored locally on your device, you remain in control of your content.

Children's Privacy

PoseCam is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Rights

You may revoke Camera or Photos permissions at any time through your device's Settings. If you uninstall the App, any data stored only by the App will be removed from your device, except for photos you have saved.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
